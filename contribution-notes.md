Community Setup Proposal for Forma Robotics

Hey Forma team, my

I’ve been checking out your open-source work and I think the project could really benefit from a small but structured community layer. It would make it easier for developers, designers, and early supporters to engage, contribute, and grow together around Forma.

Here’s a simple setup idea that won’t take much time or resources:

1. Communication Channel
Create a Discord or Telegram group focused on contributors and supporters. Add the link under a “Join the Community” section in the README.

2. Contribution Flow
Add a “#community-contributions” channel (or pinned thread) where small ideas and improvements can be discussed before people make pull requests. This helps new contributors know where to start.

3. Onboarding Docs
Add a short CONTRIBUTING.md file that explains:
	•	How to fork and create branches
	•	The best way to open PRs
	•	What kind of contributions are most useful right now

4. Optional Next Step
If it helps, I can share a quick layout on how to keep contributors engaged (like a simple update flow or recognition system).

Just thought this might help make collaboration smoother as more people discover Forma Robotics.
